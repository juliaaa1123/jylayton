J.Y. Layton

JY is a Tokyo-born artist and designer based in Los Angeles. With a background in chemical engineering and formal training in both fashion and visual art, her work bridges technical precision and emotional storytelling.

JY’s creative practice explores memory, texture, and the tension between structure and softness. Her garments, paintings, and textile works draw from dreams, nostalgia, and internal landscapes — forming a world where emotion is translated through color, material, and form. Through her work, she investigates how fashion can hold memory, and how technology can be reimagined as something deeply human.
